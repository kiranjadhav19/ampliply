Our approach

Four rules we do not bend

They are easy to write on a website and harder to hold to when a shortcut would make more money. These are the ones we hold to.

01

One job, done well

An app that does six things badly is six problems. Ours do one thing, and the first screen is the thing you came for — not a tour, a tutorial or an upsell.

02

Your data stays put

We run no servers, so there is no account, no profile and nowhere for your settings to go. They live on your phone and they are gone when you uninstall.

03

Ads with manners

Our apps are free and carry Google ads. We pace them deliberately: never over a dialog, never while you are mid‑task, never two full‑screen ads in a row.

04

Ask for less

Every permission has to earn its place and be explained in plain words before it is requested. If a feature needs something invasive, we build the feature differently.

Privacy, in one paragraph

We cannot lose data we never collect

Ampliply has no backend. Nothing you do in our apps is sent to us, because there is no “us” to send it to — no account system, no analytics of our own, no crash service, no newsletter list. The one exception is advertising: our free apps show ads through Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes an advertising ID and device information to serve and measure them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your answer from inside the app at any time.
