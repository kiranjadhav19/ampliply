Before you write

Four things that fix most of it

Not because we want to avoid the email — because you would rather have it working now.

  1. Update the app. Google Play → your library. A fix may already be out.
  2. Restart the app and your phone. Dull, but it clears most stuck states.
  3. Check battery optimisation. Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and some Samsung phones kill background apps; set ours to “Unrestricted” or “Don’t optimise”.
  4. For TV Remote, check the network. Phone and TV on the same Wi‑Fi, and not on a guest network.

What to put in your email

This gets to an answer far faster than a back‑and‑forth:

  • Which app, and its version — it is at the bottom of the app’s menu or settings
  • Your phone model and Android version
  • For TV Remote: your TV model and its year
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if there was anything to see

Please do not send us passwords, payment details or anything else sensitive. We never need them.

Common questions

Are your apps free? Is there a subscription?

Free, with no subscription and no in‑app purchases. Ads pay for them. If a paid, ad‑free option ever appears, the free version will stay.

Do I need an account to use an Ampliply app?

No. None of our apps have an account system, and none of them ask for an email address.

Which app should I ask about privacy in?

Start with the master policy, then the supplement for your app — TV Remote or Unplug. If the answer is not there, that is a gap in our writing, and we would like to know.

Can I delete the data an app has stored?

Yes, and you do not need us for it. Uninstalling removes everything, as does Android Settings → Apps → the app → Storage → Clear data.

Will you build the app I need?

Possibly. We are a small operation and we pick things we would use ourselves, but a clear description of a real annoyance is the best pitch there is — both of our apps started as exactly that.

Do you take business or partnership enquiries?

Yes — hello@ampliply.com.
