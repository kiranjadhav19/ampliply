Voice

A careful engineer explaining their own work

Plain, specific, unhurried. Concrete numbers over adjectives. Honest about the limits before anyone has to ask.

Do

  • Say the absence — “no account” lands harder than “privacy‑first”
  • Lead with the problem: Lost the remote again?
  • Name the limitation in the same breath as the claim
  • Write what happens: stored only on your phone
  • Short sentences, one idea each

Don’t

  • Revolutionary, seamless, game‑changing, supercharge, unlock
  • Exclamation marks, countdowns, “join 10,000 users”
  • Bare adjectives: secure, private, anonymous
  • “Samsung TV Remote” — the brand never leads
  • A second adjective where the first did the job

Need the files?

The marks are plain SVG and the tokens are CSS custom properties, so there is nothing to license and nothing to install.
